# Section review checklists

## Abstract
Match purpose, data, period, sample, method, results, and contribution to the body. Reduce generic background, undefined abbreviations, unsupported novelty, and causal overstatement. Check the journal limit.

## Introduction and literature review
Confirm a concrete gap, response, questions, and contributions supported by results. Remove repetition without changing the approved logic. Verify paragraph-level topic unity, evidence, competing findings, recent core literature, and links to the current study.

## Data and methodology
Confirm sources, dates, units, frequency, sample flow, preprocessing, alignment, limitations, variable/symbol definitions, procedural order, settings, splits, leakage prevention, benchmarks, metrics, tests, seeds, software, and reproducibility. Do not demand new data or replace the method merely to improve results.

## Results, discussion, and implications
Cross-check every number against tables/figures and report null results. Separate findings from interpretations. Qualify mechanisms not directly tested. Connect theoretical, methodological, and practical implications to literature-review gaps and actual findings.

## Conclusion
Answer the purpose concisely; add no new results or citations. Align contribution claims with the Abstract and Discussion and link future work to genuine limitations.
