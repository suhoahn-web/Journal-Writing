# Assembly and visual integration

- Preserve the approved paragraph sequence and sentence-level content.
- Run paragraph and sentence-count checks against the drafting ledger.
- Place each table or figure after its first substantive body reference when journal rules permit.
- Ensure numbering and body references are complete and sequential.
- Use concise titles. Put definitions, units, sample sizes, significance conventions, tests, and display rules in notes or legends.
- Do not put design-production instructions in captions.
- Make captions self-contained without repeating the Results narrative.
- Verify that color encodes an intentional, consistent distinction; also support grayscale or non-color distinction.
- Check figure geometry, alignment, arrows, labels, clipping, and panel numbering.
- Cross-check every in-text citation against the reference list and every reference-list item against the body.
- Assemble English and translated versions from the same approved source and confirm semantic equivalence.
