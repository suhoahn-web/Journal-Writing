# Assembly and visual integration

- Preserve the approved paragraph sequence and sentence-level content.
- Run paragraph and sentence-count checks against the drafting ledger.
- Place each table or figure after its first substantive body reference when journal rules permit.
- Ensure numbering and body references are complete and sequential.
- Use concise titles. Put definitions, units, sample sizes, significance conventions, tests, and display rules in notes or legends.
- Do not put design-production instructions in captions.
- Make captions self-contained without repeating the Results narrative.
- Verify that color encodes an intentional, consistent distinction; also support grayscale or non-color distinction.
- Check figure geometry, alignment, arrows, labels, clipping, and panel numbering.
- Cross-check every in-text citation against the reference list and every reference-list item against the body.
- Assemble English and translated versions from the same approved source and confirm semantic equivalence.
- Before assembly, audit every citation of the drafted sections against the literature reading records: drop citations whose paper was not obtained or whose record does not say what the sentence claims, and rewrite sentences to the record's numbers. In practice seven of about forty citations in a first draft failed this audit and one sentence stated the opposite of the paper's finding.
- Build the reference list by code from the reading records (authors, year, title, journal, volume, pages, DOI) with the version-read and indexing flags, and check by code that every entry is cited and every citation has an entry.
- Keep a drafting ledger that records, per stage, what was written, what was changed and why, and the open verify items; the ledger is the audit trail for the author and for the review stage.
- When the author reads in another language, each document or tab opens with a short reading guide in that language saying what the tab is, where the numbers come from and what the author should look at.
- Close the stage with a numbered pre-submission checklist: non-published versions to verify, journals pending indexing checks, primary sources still to cite, length against the journal norm, and experiments still empty.
