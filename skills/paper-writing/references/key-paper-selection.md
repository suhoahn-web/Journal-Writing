# Key-paper selection

Use published papers as structural evidence, not as text to imitate blindly.

1. Establish the target journal and the completed study's analytical method.
2. Search the target journal first. Rank candidates by method, empirical design, data structure, evaluation design, then topic.
3. Select different section models only when one paper cannot credibly guide the whole manuscript. Prefer one anchor paper for continuity.
4. Obtain and read the full text. Record title, journal, indexing status, section structure, paragraph count, sentence count, citation placement, tables, and figures.
5. Explain why each candidate is or is not structurally reusable for this study.
6. When presenting a paper to the user, always give its full title.
