# Section writing

## Introduction
Build a study-specific necessity chain: why the topic requires study; known problems; precise unresolved gap; the current response; principal findings; contributions; organization. The first paragraph need not be only “economic importance.” Each paragraph begins with its central claim.

## Literature review
Organize by concepts, findings, conflicts, and unresolved limits rather than listing papers. End each paragraph by connecting its evidence to the current study. Derive theoretical, methodological, and practical differentiation from verified literature.

## Data
Follow the chosen method-matched key paper's reporting order. State sources, period, frequency, units, availability timing, inclusion/exclusion, missing/outlier treatment, transformations, alignment, final sample, and limitations only as applicable.

## Methodology
Explain the actual procedure in reproducible order. Define variables, symbols, models, settings, splits, leakage controls, benchmarks, evaluation metrics, tests, seeds, software, and hardware as needed. Merge redundant paragraphs without losing distinct methodological functions.

## Results and discussion
Report results in research-question order and refer to tables/figures where first used. Separate findings from interpretation. Discuss comparison with literature, alternative explanations, unexpected or null results, theoretical/methodological/practical implications, generalizability, and limitations without overstating causality.

## Conclusion and abstract
Conclusion answers the purpose without new evidence or citations. Draft the abstract last and reconcile purpose, data, method, central results, and bounded contribution with the body and journal word limit.
